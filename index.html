About me

Statistics taught me to interrogate evidence. UX gave that instinct a human focus.

I entered design through an unconventional route: statistics. That training shaped how I work today — testing assumptions, looking for patterns and staying close to evidence. I am most interested in problems where behaviour, systems and technology overlap.
